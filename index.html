What is Girlsgiving? 🤔

Glad you asked, babe

Girlsgiving
noun · /ˈɡɜːlz-ɡɪv-ɪŋ/

1An annual holiday where a group of fabulous women gather to eat far too much, give thoughtful gifts, gossip responsibly (mostly), and remind each other how loved they are. 💕

2Basically Thanksgiving's cuter little sister 💅🏽: same cosy feast, way better outfits, zero awkward uncles asking when you're settling down, and a strict "nobody leaves without a plate" policy. The turkey is negotiable. The hugs are not. 🦃🤗
